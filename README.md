# 🎼 SONARIUM

> *“If emotions had colors, Sonarium would be their sound.”*

---

## 🎧 What is Sonarium?

**Sonarium** is an experimental, synesthetic framework for mapping **emotion to sound**, built on the belief that every human feeling exists as a coordinate within a complex mood space—one that can be expressed **not just in color, but in tone, pitch, timbre, and resonance**.

Imagine a **color picker**, but for emotion. Now imagine that instead of selecting colors, you're selecting **emotional states**, which Sonarium interprets as **layered sound textures**, **ambient tones**, and **musical expressions**. Whether you're feeling nostalgic, anxious, euphoric, or serene—Sonarium can find that frequency, and **play your soul back to you**.

---

## 🌀 The Core Idea

Where color uses **RGB** or **hex** to express visual states, Sonarium introduces an emotional bit-field—an abstract coordinate system spanning:

- **Valence** (positive ↔ negative)
- **Arousal** (calm ↔ energetic)
- **Tension** (stable ↔ unstable)
- **Focus** (clarity ↔ chaos)
- **Depth** (surface ↔ profound)
- **Warmth** (comfort ↔ alienation)

Encoded into a **64-bit mood vector**, Sonarium renders these psychological fingerprints as **soundscapes**, using musical scale transitions, pitch modulation, tone layering, and frequency shifts. Each "sound hex" becomes a way to **transmit emotion without words**—a tool for musicians, psychologists, creatives, or AI artists exploring affective computing.

---

## 🌸 Mood Visualization

Inspired by **Plutchik’s wheel of emotion**, Sonarium visualizes emotions as **flower-shaped UI clusters**, where:
- **Angle** = mood type
- **Distance from center** = intensity
- **Each petal** = tonal family (major/minor, consonance/dissonance)
- **Hexagonal/Pentagonal inner layers** = deeper emotional structure

This results in a **visceral interface** that lets users **pick feelings** like frequencies—by intuition, not labels.

---

## 🌐 Why Sonarium?

Because emotions are **not binary**. They're not just "happy" or "sad". They exist in gradients, swirls, textures—just like sound. Sonarium doesn't aim to simplify emotion—it captures its **complexity** and **expresses it through sonic design**, giving rise to:

- 🧠 **Mood-based music generation**
- 💬 **Emotion-responsive UI/UX systems**
- 🎨 **Cross-sensory creativity (sound ↔ color ↔ feeling)**
- 🧘🏽 **Biofeedback tools for therapy or mindfulness**
- 🪄 **Ambient storytelling & generative art installations**
